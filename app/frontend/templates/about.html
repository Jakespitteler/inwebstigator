About Inwebstigator

What it does

Inwebstigator watches the websites you choose and tells you when they change, so you don't have to keep checking them yourself.

  • It checks each website on a schedule (every day by default).
  • It reads the website's main page and any critical pages you choose, and maps the other pages on the site.
  • It spots changed, added and removed text, links and documents (such as PDFs), and pages added to or removed from the site.
  • Changes appear on the dashboard's Updates tab and are emailed to the website's notification addresses.
  • If nothing changes, it sends a short "no changes" email (every 7 days by default), so you know it is still running.

Your list of websites and everything found about them is stored on this computer.
